In 590, a plague was killing Rome, and the city looked up to see an angel on an emperor's tomb, putting away his sword. Who is Saint Michael the Archangel? What does the Bible really say about him, and what is only tradition? From the Book of Daniel and the Apocalypse to Monte Gargano, Mont Saint-Michel, Joan of Arc and the Saint Michael prayer of Pope Leo XIII.
